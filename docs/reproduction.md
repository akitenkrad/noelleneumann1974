# Reproduction

[English](reproduction.md) | [日本語](reproduction.ja.md)

The paper is an empirical study, not an ABM, so reproduction compares the model's **steady state** (the second-half-of-run average, since the survey numbers are cross-sections while the ABM produces a dynamic equilibrium) against the Allensbach-survey anchors of Tables 1–5, using an ABM→survey translation for each.

Run it end-to-end:

```bash
cargo run --release -- reproduce --seed 42      # one runvault run under results/noelleneumann/
uv run noelleneumann-tools reproduce            # prints the PASS / off-anchor table
```

## The anchors

| Anchor | Paper value | Metric | Target band |
|--------|-------------|--------|-------------|
| Train-test willingness to discuss (Table 1) | 36% | `steady_voice_volume` | 0.30–0.42 |
| Winning camp voicing (Table 2) | 53% | `final_majority_voice` | 0.45–0.65 |
| Losing camp voicing (Table 2) | 28% | `final_minority_voice` | 0.15–0.40 |
| **Majority/minority asymmetry (H1 core)** | OR ≈ 2.9 | `steady_majority_voice_ratio` (log-OR) | **> 0.8** |
| Current-climate perception (Table 4) | 49% / 43% | `final_pi_now_pro` / `final_pi_now_con` | 0.40–0.55 / 0.40–0.60 |
| **Future-assessment gap (H5)** | 70% vs 23% | `steady_future_assessment_gap` | **> 0.4** |
| **Hardcore boundary** | 17–25% minority voice more than the majority | `boundary_hardcore_survival` (`hardcore_frac=0.25`) | **> 0.7** |

The "paper value" column and the "target band" column live in different files, on purpose. The paper's own numbers — the Table 1 / 2 / 4 percentages — go to the run's `reference.csv`, each with the source it was read from. The bands, and the three anchors that have no paper number at all (H1, H5, hardcore), are this replication's own and go to `events.jsonl` as `x.noelleneumann1974.anchor`. Merged into one row they would stop being distinguishable, which is what `reference.csv` exists to prevent.

## How the metrics are defined

- **`majority_voice_ratio`** is the log-odds-ratio of the majority camp's own-side voicing odds to the minority camp's. Defining it by *role* (majority vs minority) rather than by sign means it reads positive regardless of which sign happens to be the majority. A positive log-OR is the spiral-of-silence signature (H1).
- **`future_assessment_gap`** is the majority's mean future-climate perception minus the minority's. The majority stays optimistic about its side's future while the minority turns pessimistic (H5).
- **`hardcore_survival`** is the share of the *minority hardcore* (low-threshold, mobilized minority agents) that keep voicing their own side. Raising `hardcore_frac` produces a vocal minority that resists the spiral — the paper's "17–25% minority more willing to speak than a 53–61% majority."
- **`perceived_minus_actual`** (`q̂ − q`) is the divergence between the apparent public support among speakers and the true private support — the emergent H3 distortion.

The three bold anchors are the model's calibration targets. The absolute Table-1/2/4 percentage bands are guidance: because the ABM runs to a dynamic equilibrium rather than a survey snapshot, the absolute voicing levels can sit above the survey cross-section while the *asymmetry* (the log-OR) and the *future gap* land squarely in band.

## Scenarios

`reproduce` runs two configurations from one seed base:

1. **Socialism scenario** (`q=0.37`, `eta_m=0.5`, `alpha=0.7`, `hardcore_frac=0.05`) — the winning/losing-camp asymmetry and the H1/H5 anchors.
2. **Hardcore-boundary scenario** (`hardcore_frac=0.25`) — the hardcore-survival anchor.
